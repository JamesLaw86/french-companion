Why this site exists

Courses are good at teaching you French in order. They are bad at being the thing you reach for six months later when you think “wait — when is it j’avais été and not j’ai été?” or “what does du coup actually mean, and why does everyone say it every ten seconds?”

This site is that thing. Every page is written to be re-readable: short explanations in plain English, lots of examples, and the tricks that make real conversation possible before your grammar is perfect.
